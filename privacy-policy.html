Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SQUIC LOANS handles information you choose to submit through this website, including customer loan enquiries and partner registration forms.

1. Information we may collect

We may collect your name, mobile number, location, email address, loan enquiry type and the information you choose to include in a message or partner registration form.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to your enquiry and contact you about the service requested.
  • To understand your loan requirement and explain possible next steps.
  • To review partner registration enquiries and maintain enquiry records.
  • To protect the website and prevent misuse.

3. Sharing with lenders or service partners

Where appropriate, information may be shared with a relevant lender or service partner to progress your enquiry, with your knowledge or consent where required. The lender may apply its own privacy policy and eligibility checks. We do not sell your personal information as a product.

4. Sensitive information

Do not submit passwords, OTPs, card PINs, net-banking credentials or other authentication secrets through this website. Submit identity, income or bank documents only through a verified channel when legitimately requested by the relevant lender.

5. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to limit access to enquiry information. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Third-party services

Forms may be processed by third-party website form services, and links may take you to WhatsApp or a lender's website. Those services have their own privacy practices.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about, correct or request deletion of information you submitted, subject to applicable legal, business-record and processing requirements.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact SQUIC LOANS at squicloans.com or 82484 18691 .

This is a general website privacy notice. Please review it for your actual data-handling practices and obtain legal advice if needed before publishing.
